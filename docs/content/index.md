# Chetna

An app to help Indian parents get their children's screen time under control — not just track it, but actively enforce it.

## Why

Excess smartphone use among children is widespread in India, and most existing tools only measure the problem (digital wellbeing dashboards) rather than solve it (proactively blocking access). This spans more than one situation — a tween sneaking their own phone, and a toddler handed a parent's phone who ends up glued to it for hours are different problems needing different solutions. See [Competitive landscape & feasibility](planning/research/competitive-landscape.md) for the research, and [the tentative v1 feature set](brainstorm/v1-feature-set.md) for where the scoping conversation currently stands.
